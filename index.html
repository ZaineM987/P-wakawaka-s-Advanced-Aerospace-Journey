Educator Notes: Aerospace & Pedagogy

This application integrates 50 Advanced Aerospace Concepts designed to gently introduce the foundations of Space Exploration and Engineering to 4-to-6-year-olds.

It utilizes Cognitive Load Theory (CLT) by John Sweller to make advanced concepts accessible:

  • Minimizing Extraneous Load: Interfaces are stripped of unnecessary text. The scrolling layout enforces a vertical flex-column with heavy padding to ensure perfect tactile mobile behavior.
  • Managing Intrinsic Load: Complex technical ideas (e.g., Aerodynamics, Lift, Orbits, Black Holes, Exoplanets) are reduced to highly relatable, physical metaphors (e.g., "slicing the wind", "invisible strings", "circle games").
  • Modality Effect: The Text-to-Speech (TTS) feature prevents working memory overload by offloading reading demands to the auditory channel.
